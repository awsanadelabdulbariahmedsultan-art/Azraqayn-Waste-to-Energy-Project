* **Final Product:** Uniform plastic granules (pellets) with a 3 mm diameter, ready for direct sale to local pipe and bag factories in Sana'a.

### D. Color & Polymer Optical Sorters (Future Expansion Phase)
* **Objective:** Sort PET flakes by color and polymer type with up to 99.9% accuracy to produce food-grade recycled plastic for international export markets.

## 4️⃣ Alternative Fuel (RDF) & Compressed Biogas (Bio-CNG) Lines (Future Expansion)

### A. Refuse-Derived Fuel (RDF) Production Line
* **Equipment Required:** Primary shredders, high-speed secondary fine shredders, thermal drying station utilizing recovered heat from generators, and hydraulic baling/pelletizing presses.
* **Commercial Objective:** Processing non-recyclable dry waste into high-calorific RDF blocks to sell to major cement plants (e.g., Amran Cement Factory) as a cost-effective alternative to coal and diesel.

### B. Advanced Biogas Upgrading System
* **Technology:** Membrane Separation Technology or Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA) to extract carbon dioxide and isolate biomethane with a purity exceeding 97%.

### C. Bio-CNG Compression & Filling Station
* **Equipment Required:** High-pressure gas compressors (up to 250 bar), ground steel cylinder storage cascades, and specialized gas fuel dispensers (Bio-CNG Dispensers).
* **Commercial Objective:** Bottling pure methane into cylinders to sell as a clean, highly economical alternative fuel for transport trucks, public buses, and taxis in Sana'a city.
